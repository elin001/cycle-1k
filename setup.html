🚴 Strava Setup

Each rider authorizes once. Paste your Strava app's Client ID below, click Authorize, then copy the refresh token you get back and add it to Vercel.

Find your Client ID at strava.com/settings/api.

After both tokens are saved in Vercel as STRAVA_USER1_REFRESH_TOKEN and STRAVA_USER2_REFRESH_TOKEN, redeploy — you're done.
